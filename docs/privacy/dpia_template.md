# Data-protection impact assessment (DPIA) — template

> **DRAFT — to be checked by lawyers.**

Article 35 of the GDPR asks for an assessment before processing that is likely to be a high risk to people. Health data (Article 9) is such a case. **Care services and benefit claims may not start until the board has filled this in and signed it** (docs/08, section 7). Fill in every box in plain words. "Not applicable" needs a reason.

Do one assessment for care services and one for benefit claims, or one for both if the data and the steps are the same.

## 1. Description

1. What will members be able to do? (One short paragraph, no technical words.)
2. What data is used? List each item; say which is health-related. (Use [the data map](data_map.md).)
3. Whose data is it? About how many people at the start, and in a year?
4. Who will see the data, at each step? (The member, the random confirmers, holders, operators.)
5. Where does the data live, how is it protected, and how long is it kept?
6. Does any data leave Unicorn? To whom, and why?

## 2. Why it is necessary

1. What is the purpose? Which rule of the plan needs it?
2. Is there a way to reach the purpose with less data? (For example: only a yes/no, never a diagnosis.) Why was that not enough?
3. What is the legal basis (6(1)(b) and 9(2)(a))? How is explicit consent asked for, and how can it be withdrawn?
4. How do members see, correct and delete their data? Does deleting a claim also delete its documents?

## 4. Measures
