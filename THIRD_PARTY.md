## Software the server runs

The Python packages Unicorn uses (Django, django-allauth, django-htmx, django-csp, django-environ, cryptography, PyYAML, numpy, scipy, gunicorn, mysqlclient and others) are open-source software under their own licences. They are listed with their versions in `requirements.txt`.
