# Terms of use

**DRAFT — to be written with lawyers (docs/12, section 2).** This text is a first version for the lawyers to correct.

## What Unicorn is

Unicorn is an app with which members plan their economy together. Every hour of work pays the same, and prices are the hours of work inside a product. UNIQ cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for any currency.

## Unicorn is not a bank

Unicorn never moves money and never reads bank accounts. It shows payment requests, and the person who pays and the person who receives confirm them. Euros that members hold for the group stay in the holders' own accounts.

## Rules of use

- One person has one account. Please do not make a second one.
- Be honest about the hours you work and the things you offer. Offers of illegal goods or services are not allowed, and trades that need a licence are open only to members with a verified licence.
- Treat other members with respect. Harassment and sharing other people's private data are not allowed.
- The decisions of the members, the random confirmers and the councils follow the rules shown on the Rules page. Everything is a ticket, and every ticket keeps its history.

## Reporting something wrong (notice and action)

Any member can report an offer, a comment, a ticket, a profile, hours, a review or something else that seems illegal, wrong or harmful. Members drawn by chance look at the report and decide. If something is hidden, its author gets a statement of reasons: what was reported, who decided, how, and how to contest it. The author can ask for a panel of 12 other members to look again. Anyone can also write to <to fill in>.

## Selling to people outside Unicorn

Members who sell to outside customers sell in their own name. They give the invoice, answer to the customer under consumer law, and keep their own set-aside for taxes.

## Changes

These terms change only with a new release of the app and after the members have had the chance to read them. The current version is always at the address of this page.
