## What we keep, why, and on what basis

- **Your email address, your display name, your country and the area you live in (the postcode only).** To let you log in, to show other members who they work with, and to work out local councils and delivery areas. Basis: the membership contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Your legal name, address, phone number, date of birth and bank account.** To check your identity, to pay and receive money between members, and for the tax paperwork. They are encrypted with a key that belongs to you alone. Basis: the membership contract and legal duties (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).
- **Your hours, orders, bookings, tickets, comments and votes as a confirmer.** To run the books and the decisions of the members. The books must be kept by law and cannot be changed. Basis: the contract and legal duties.
- **Health-related claims.** Only if you ask for a benefit that needs it. Then the members who look at it see a confirmation, never a diagnosis. Basis: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).
- **Your consent to be helped.** If a helper acts for you, we keep the consent and a record of what the helper did. Basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
- **Logs.** To find errors and attacks. They hold no personal data except a shortened internet address. Basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Secret ballots hold no member: nobody, not even the people who run Unicorn, can say how you voted.

## How long

- Your data stays until you delete your account. Then the key to your private data is destroyed, your name and email address are removed, and your member number stays in the books, because the books must be kept and cannot be changed.
- Contact details of outside customers are deleted 90 days after delivery. Invoices are kept as long as the law asks: <to fill in>.
- Backups are kept for up to 12 months. After a restore, deletions are done again. Encrypted data in an old backup stays unreadable once the key is destroyed.

## Who sees your data

Only the people named in the data map: you, the members who work with you or who are drawn to look at something you did, the holders of the trade fund for payments, and the few people who run the server. Nothing is sent to anyone outside the European Union. We use no tracking, no advertising and no outside fonts or analytics.

You can see and download all your data (My account, Download all my data), correct it, and delete your account.
